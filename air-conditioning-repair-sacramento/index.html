What an AC repair visit may involve

A repair starts with understanding the symptoms and assessing the equipment. The exact steps depend on what the system is doing, so there isn’t one checklist that fits every call. A contractor may need to inspect the system, identify the cause, and explain the repair options before work proceeds.

Before calling, jot down a few details:

  • Is the system not turning on, or is it running without cooling?
  • When did you first notice the problem?
  • Have you heard a new sound or noticed anything else unusual?
  • Is the thermostat displaying a setting or message you can share?

Avoid guessing at the cause or repeatedly restarting a system that seems to be malfunctioning. Those details are more useful to a technician than an attempted do-it-yourself repair.
