What to prepare for an AC installation

The installation process depends on the property and the equipment being installed. A contractor needs to understand the existing setup and the home’s cooling needs before outlining the work. The project may involve removing or replacing existing equipment, installing the new system, and checking its operation. Ask the contractor to explain which steps apply to your home.

Before you call, make a short list:

  • Is this a replacement or a first-time installation?
  • What do you know about the current system?
  • Which rooms or areas are you hoping to cool?
  • Are there concerns you want the contractor to consider during the assessment?

Try not to choose equipment based only on a model you saw elsewhere. A system needs to be considered in relation to your home and its existing setup. Ask questions if a recommended option or part of the installation isn’t clear.
