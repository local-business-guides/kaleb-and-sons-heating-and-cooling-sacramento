What happens when you request emergency repair

Start with a short, specific description. Say whether the issue affects heating, air conditioning, or both. Share the equipment’s location, the property type, and any changes you noticed just before it stopped working. If the system still operates but isn’t working as expected, describe that too.

The contractor may need to assess the equipment before identifying the cause. Once the issue is understood, ask what repair is recommended, whether any additional work is being proposed, and what it will involve. Make sure you understand the explanation before approving work.
