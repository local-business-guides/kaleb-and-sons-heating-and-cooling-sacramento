What a repair visit may cover

A technician will need to understand the reported symptoms and look at the equipment to determine what may be wrong. The repair needed depends on the system and the issue found. You can make the conversation more useful by preparing a few details:

  • Which part of the home or building is affected?
  • When did you first notice the problem?
  • Does the system turn on, and does it run continuously or stop and start?
  • Have you noticed a noise, smell, or leak?
  • Has anything changed in the system’s performance?

Keep the area around the equipment accessible if you can do so safely. If you’re unsure whether something is safe to touch, leave it alone and tell the technician about it instead. Ask for an explanation of the issue and the recommended repair before work proceeds.
